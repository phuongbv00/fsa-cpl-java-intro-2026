# Topic 07: Recruitment Platform

## From: Mark Cuban (mark@talentbridge.io)

## To: Tech Team

## Subject: Fixing our Hiring Process

Team,

We are TalentBridge, a recruitment agency. We match companies with tech talent.

The problem is, our process is entirely manual. Companies email us job descriptions, we post them on LinkedIn, we get resumes via email, and then forward them back to the companies. It’s chaotic. Resume files get lost, candidates don't know if they got the job or not, and I'm losing my mind.

I need a platform where companies can post their own jobs. Candidates should be able to create a profile, upload their CV, and apply directly. My team should just oversee it—screen candidates, move them to "Interview" stage, or "Reject" them.

For the candidates, maybe let them import their LinkedIn profile so they don't have to type everything again? That would be smooth.

Also, we get so many resumes. If there was a way for the system to read the PDF and tell us "This guy knows Java", that would be a game changer. And maybe we could have them record a short video introduction? Companies love that.

Let me know.

Mark
