# Topic 09: Library Management System

## From: Mrs. Phelps (phelpsi@citylibrary.gov)

## To: IT Department

## Subject: Managing the Library

Hello,

I'm Mrs. Phelps, the head librarian at Central City Library. We have thousands of books, but our card catalog system is ancient.

Students come in looking for a book, and I have to physically check the shelf to see if it's there. Borrowing is done by writing names in a ledger book. Calculating overdue fines is a nightmare—I have to count the days on a calendar!

We need a computer system. I want to put all our books in a database—Title, Author, ISBN. Students should be able to search on a computer and see if a book is available or checked out.

When they borrow a book, I want to just scan it (maybe a barcode?) and have the system record it against their name. If they bring it back late, the system should tell me how much they owe.

Also, I've heard about OCR... could we scan old rare books and make them searchable digitally? That might be too ambitious, but it's a thought. And automated recommendations? "If you liked Harry Potter, try Percy Jackson"?

Please help us modernize.

Mrs. Phelps
