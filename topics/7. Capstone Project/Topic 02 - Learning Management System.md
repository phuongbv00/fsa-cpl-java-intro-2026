# Topic 02: Learning Management System

## From: Sarah Jenkins (sarah.j@brightpath.edu)

## To: Dev Team

## Subject: Chaos at BrightPath Academy - HELP!

Hello developers,

I'm writing to you because I'm about to lose my mind. I run BrightPath Academy, and we have about 500 students now. It's great, but managing everything on Excel and Google Drive is killing us.

My instructors are emailing PDFs to students, students are emailing assignments back (sometimes to the wrong email), and I'm manually entering grades into a spreadsheet. Last week, a student complained that he never got the materials for Week 3, and I had no way to prove we sent them.

We need a portal. A place where students can log in, see their courses, and download the slides and videos. They should be able to upload their homework there too, so no more "lost emails". Instructors should be able to grade it right there.

Also, I need to see who's actually teaching what and who the students are. Maybe different logins for students, teachers, and me (admin)?

I've seen some other sites where you can do live video classes right inside the website. Can we do that? Or maybe generate a certificate automatically when they finish? That would save me hours of printing. Oh, and quizzes! If we could have online quizzes that grade themselves, my teachers would love you forever.

Let's chat soon.

Best,
Sarah Jenkins
Director, BrightPath Academy
