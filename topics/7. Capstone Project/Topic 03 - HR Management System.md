# Topic 03: HR Management System

## From: Linda Vance (linda.vance@globaltech.inc)

## To: Dev Team

## Subject: HR System Modernization

Dear Tech Partner,

I am the HR Manager at GlobalTech. We have around 150 employees now, and our manual processes are becoming a bottleneck.

Our biggest headache is leave management. Currently, an employee sends me an email to ask for leave, I forward it to their manager, the manager replies (eventually), and then I record it in Excel. It's slow and prone to error. We need a system where they can just click "Request Leave", the manager clicks "Approve", and it's done.

We also need to track attendance. Just a simple check-in/out button when they start work would suffice for now. At the end of the month, I need to be able to pull a report to calculate salaries. Speaking of salaries, if the system could give me a rough calculation based on their attendance, that would be helpful.

Of course, we need to keep employee details safe—addresses, phone numbers, etc.

I was reading about AI recently... is it possible to use a webcam for attendance? Like face recognition? That would stop people from checking in for their friends. Also, we get hundreds of resumes; can the system read them and tell me who has the right skills? And maybe sync with our Outlook calendars?

Looking forward to your proposal.

Regards,
Linda Vance
HR Manager
