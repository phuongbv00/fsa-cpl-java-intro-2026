# Topic 10: Food Ordering System

## From: Gordon Ramsay (gordon@yummybites.com)

## To: Tech Team

## Subject: ORDERING SYSTEM NOW!

Listen,

I'm Gordon from YummyBites. We make great food, but our ordering process is a disaster. The phone rings non-stop during lunch. We write down orders, the kitchen can't read the handwriting, we get the order wrong, the customer yells at us. It's chaos!

I need an online ordering app. Similar to UberEats but just for us. Customers should see our menu—nice photos, descriptions, prices. They need to be able to customize—"No onions", "Extra spicy", that sort of thing.

They add to cart, choose delivery or pickup, and place the order.

In the kitchen, I want a screen that pops up new orders instantly. We cook it, mark it as "Ready", and then the driver takes it. The customer should see the status change on their phone so they stop calling us asking "Where is my food?!".

Maybe later we can track the driver on a map? And voice ordering? That would be cool. Also, group ordering—office workers always want to split the bill.

Get it done.

Gordon
